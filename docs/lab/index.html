About us

Our research develops scalable statistical methods for high-dimensional and dependent data. We connect statistical theory, computation, and domain knowledge to study questions across disciplines.

Led by Yizhou (Jake) Cai in the Department of Mathematics at Texas State University, our work spans inference, latent-variable models, structural changes, and interdisciplinary data science.

Interested in collaborating or pursuing graduate research? We welcome a conversation about your interests.
