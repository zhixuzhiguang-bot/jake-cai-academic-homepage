About Us

HETU Lab develops scalable statistical methods for high-dimensional and dependent data. We connect statistical theory, computation, and domain knowledge to study questions across disciplines.

Led by Yizhou (Jake) Cai in the Department of Mathematics at Texas State University, our work spans inference, latent-variable models, structural changes, and interdisciplinary data science.

If you are interested in joining our team, send us an email.

Research Areas

Statistical Methodology

Statistical Inference

Scalable estimation and approximation for models with latent quantities and complex dependence.

High-dimensional & Dependent Data

Methods for spatial, spatiotemporal, ordinal, and other structured observations.

Change-point & Causal Methods

Identifying structural changes and studying causal questions in multivariate and evolving data.

Interdisciplinary Research

Public Health & Mental Health

Health-related information, social media, and changes in mental-health trajectories.

Finance & Insurance

Insurance claims, spatial risk, and dependence modeling.

Education

Collaborative statistical research on questions in education.

AI & Society

Generative AI adoption, visual-AI bias, and the use of AI in academic practice.
