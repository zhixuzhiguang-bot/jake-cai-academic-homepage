Director, HETU Lab

Jake develops scalable statistical methods for high-dimensional and dependent data. His research spans causal inference, structural changes, and interdisciplinary applications in public health, finance, education, and AI assessment.

Dr. Yun (Erica) Yang

Collaborator (Data Scientist), HETU Lab

Erica Yang is an independent researcher and data scientist who earned her Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of South Carolina. Her research interests include statistical machine learning, Bayesian hierarchical modeling, and rearrangement-based clustering of multi-view ordinal data, with an emphasis on theoretical recovery guarantees and robust statistical inference. She also has industry experience developing predictive machine learning models and evaluating their reliability in real-world applications.

Lu Zhou

Graduate Researcher
